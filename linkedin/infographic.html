Voltage Drop

IEC · NEC · BS
1
The Formula
Vd = k · ρ · L · I / A
k = 2 single-phase, √3 three-phase
ρ = resistivity (Ω·mm²/m)
L = one-way run length (m)
I = design current (A)
A = conductor area (mm²)

4
Derating
  • Ca — ambient air temperature
  • Cg — grouping of circuits
  • Cs — soil thermal resistivity
  • Cd — depth of burial
  • Ch — triplen harmonics
5
Sizing Sequence
  • 01Design current Ib from connected load
  • 05Select cable → It from tables
  • 02Device rating In ≥ Ib
  • 06Check voltage drop ≤ limit
  • 03Apply factors Ca · Cg · …
  • 07Check thermal: k²S² ≥ I²t
  • 04Required Iz ≥ In / (Ca · Cg)
  • 08Check earth fault loop Zs
6
Where Designs Go Wrong
  • Sizing on 20 °C ρ, then ignoring conductor temperature rise
  • Skipping grouping factor when circuits share a tray or conduit
  • Confirming voltage drop but never the thermal withstand
  • Forgetting Zs — the cable trips, but the breaker never does
  • Mixing one-way and total length in the drop calc
  • Upsizing the cable to fix drop when the real issue is length
