Single-Line Diagram Builder

Click a component on the left to drop it on the canvas — there are no modes to learn. To connect two things by hand, drag a ● dot onto another symbol — it snaps for you, so you don't need to aim precisely. The canvas is unlimited: pan with Space-drag or middle-drag and zoom out freely, and the grid follows you.
